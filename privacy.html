Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

"carfeed" is a private tool used only by the owner of the Hood Secrets YouTube channel to manage that one channel.

What it accesses

Using the YouTube Data API and YouTube Analytics API, carfeed accesses only the owner's own YouTube channel, to upload videos, update video details, read channel and video statistics, and read and reply to comments on the owner's own videos.

What it does not do

carfeed does not collect, store, sell or share personal data from any other user. It is not offered to the public, and no other person signs in to it.

Storage

Access tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer and are never published or shared.

carfeed's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
